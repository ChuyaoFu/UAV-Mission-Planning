Portable algorithm changes and current limits

Implemented modules (no truth, CAD geometry or simulator API inputs):
  path_tracking.py: continuous closest-path projection, bounded progress,
    curve speed limits and collision-checked clearance smoothing. The grid
    can come from a declared prior or a real occupancy map.
  motion_limits.py: explicit stopping distance, measurement latency,
    uncertainty and bounded acceleration. Deceleration=1 m/s^2 is an
    engineering assumption, not a measured official-airframe capability.
  camera_geometry.py: consumes the calibration of the ACTUAL rectified
    image pair. HARDWARE_MODE=1 refuses the default simulator calibration.
  stereo_quality.py: independent left-right and photometric validation.
    CPU SGBM is the simulation front end, not a claimed OAK implementation.
  height_odometry.py: single downward ToF height corrections applied once
    at their own timestamps, with tilt/surface-transition gates. Assumes
    locally flat continuous floor; obstacle/ramp handling remains limited.
  mapping_cloud.py: exposure-time interpolation and finite depth uncertainty.
  portability_check.py: read-only MCAP processing/observation budget audit.

The actual mission executable retains its SITL-only arming guard. This is
intentional: these tests do not authorize or certify autonomous real flight.
start_sim.sh/start.sh are simulation harnesses, including model-specific PX4
rotor allocation/thrust settings. NEVER apply their rotor/thrust parameters
to a physical airframe. A target launch must use its measured actuator and
flight-controller configuration and real sensor drivers.

Inputs to replace/measure on the target:
- Rectified mono calibration at the published resolution, stereo baseline,
  camera-to-IMU and ToF-to-body transforms, exposure/IMU clock offset. Current
  simulation has global-shutter ideal pinhole images and exact timestamps.
- OAK-D S2 native LR-check/subpixel/confidence output. Preserve invalid depth;
  do not hole-fill low-texture walls for avoidance or SLAM. Native hardware
  depth must be compared with real recordings before replacing CPU SGBM.
- Publish original exposure timestamps, IMU, mono pair and downward range
  onboard. Independent desktop truth/CAD evaluators must remain disconnected.
- Re-measure end-to-end age, dropped frames, VIO update counts and CPU/thermal
  load on Orin. Desktop SGBM timing is NOT an Orin benchmark.
- Identify conservative braking acceleration and actual envelope. Start at a
  lower cruise speed and re-estimate the stopping bound from measured flight.
  The current 0.70 m/s cruise and 1 m/s^2 deceleration are simulation values.
- Validate vibration, motion blur, exposure changes, dark tunnel and ToF
  reflectance failures. Passive S2 stereo cannot assume active IR texture.

Portable read-only tests:
  python3 test_motion_limits.py
  python3 test_path_tracking.py
  python3 test_stereo_quality.py
  python3 test_height_odometry.py
  python3 portability_check.py ORIGINAL_MCAP OUTPUT.json

Original physical environment, vehicle collision shape, passive sensor model,
texture and judge boundaries stay unchanged in this optimization campaign.
No previous-flight map is loaded for subsequent runs. Published CAD is a
navigation prior only, never substituted for measured SLAM geometry.
